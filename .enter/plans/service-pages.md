# Service pages + gallery layout

## What this delivers

Six service pages, matching the six service pages on cbsmasonry.com, all sharing one consistent, tidy layout built around a strong project gallery:

1. Concrete Construction — `/services/concrete-construction`
2. Paving Stone & Interlocking Brick — `/services/paving-stone-interlocking-brick`
3. Natural Rock & Stone — `/services/natural-rock-stone`
4. Chimney Works — `/services/chimney-works`
5. Excavation — `/services/excavation`
6. AquaPave® System — `/services/aquapave` (already live; copy, video and diagram stay, layout and gallery brought in line with the other five)

Copy is taken from the live site: each page's headline and intro, its sub-sections (stamped concrete and exposed aggregate; paving stone; natural rock; chimney repairs, restoration & rebuilding, re-pointing & re-lining plus the "questions to ask" checklist; small/large-scale excavation, waterproofing, retaining walls, back filling), a Before & After comparison, its project gallery, then the existing call-to-action banner, BBB block and footer.

## Page layout (the same on all six)

- Full-width photo hero with the page title, one-line subtitle and the "Back to Home" link, using the page's own hero photo when set.
- Intro section: heading, short lead paragraph and the page's key points as a bulleted list beside a supporting image or video — so each page reads as one coherent service rather than a wall of text.
- Sub-sections for each distinct service on that page, laid out in alternating text/photo rows.
- Before & After comparison: two photos shown as a labelled pair, chosen for that page in the media library.
- Project gallery (see below).
- Existing call-to-action, BBB and footer blocks close every page, and the quote form still opens from the header on every page.

## Gallery behaviour (the main layout work)

- Tiles appear in small batches rather than all at once, with a **Show more** control and a count of how many project photos the page holds — so a page with 60+ photos stays quick to open.
- Photos load lazily as the visitor scrolls, with fixed tile shapes so nothing jumps while images arrive.
- Full-screen viewer for any photo, with previous/next, arrow-key support, ESC to close and a position indicator (e.g. 4 / 38), instead of the current open-and-close single image.
- Responsive: two columns on phones, three on tablets, four on desktop, with slight tile-size variation for rhythm while keeping every photo uncropped-looking and aligned.
- Identical look and behaviour on all six pages, so the site feels like one product.
- A page whose category has no photos yet shows the section with a short "photos are being added" note instead of broken or stock imagery; everything else on the page stays intact. If the photo library can't be reached, the same short message appears and the rest of the page still renders.

## Getting to the pages

- Header gains a **Services** menu listing all six pages on desktop (dropdown) and inside the mobile menu.
- Home page "Our Services" section lists the six service pages as cards — same card styling and photos as today, each one opening its page. The three generic Residential / Commercial / Specialty cards are replaced by these six.
- Footer service links point at the real pages (AquaPave already does).

## Managing photos for the pages

- Each page draws its gallery from its own upload category, exactly like AquaPave today. Concrete Construction is the only missing category and gets added; the other five already exist (Paving Stone, Natural Rock, Chimney Works, Excavation, AquaPave).
- Uploading under a category immediately places those photos on the matching page; the star still controls home page gallery placement.
- The media library's page-image panel is grouped by service page and now covers: hero photo for each of the six pages, plus a Before and After photo for each. Picking a spot and then clicking a photo assigns it, as it does today; the AquaPave diagram spot stays.

## Unchanged

- Home page hero video, About, home page gallery, reviews and BBB sections.
- AquaPave page copy, video and system diagram content.
- Upload tab and media library behaviour beyond the added category and the grouped page-image panel (grid/list views, filters, bulk featuring, moving between categories, refresh).
- Photos uploaded but not placed on a page or featured on home remain reachable by direct link but are not shown anywhere on the site.
